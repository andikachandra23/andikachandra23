A quiet problem, and a very large one.

Every school day, millions of students finish work that no one has time to read closely. Teachers do not lack care. They lack hours. The result is feedback that arrives late, and understanding that never quite forms.

AI can now read a mistake, ask the right question and draft the feedback, while the teacher stays the final voice. Schools are ready for that kind of help. Few teams are building it for crowded rooms, shared devices and weak connections. That is where we work.

What new capital builds.

Every part of the next round is aimed at one outcome: more students getting better feedback, from teachers who trust the tool.

Better models

Stronger Tutor, Marking and Insight engines in Bahasa Indonesia and regional contexts, tested against what teachers actually grade.

More schools

Structured pilots in real classrooms, measured on learning outcomes, so every new school is evidence for the next one.

Trust and safety

Student data protection, audit trails and the teacher approval step that sits on every AI draft. Trust is the product.

The right team
